Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Thresh Labs LLC is a Missouri limited liability company that designs and builds consumer software. This page describes, in general terms, how we approach privacy across our website and our products.

Our approach

We build financial software, which means we take data handling seriously. Our products are designed so that the people using them stay in control of their own information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

This website

This site is a company information page. We do not require an account to view it and we do not collect personal information from visitors beyond standard, aggregate server and traffic data used to keep the site running and understand general usage.

If you email us, we receive the contents of your message and your email address so we can reply. We use that information for correspondence only.

Our products

Our software products are in active development and are not yet generally available. A complete, product-specific privacy policy — covering the categories of data collected, how it is stored, any third-party processing, retention periods, and how to request deletion — will be published here before any product is made available to the public, and will be presented to users at signup.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page as our products develop. The “last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to legal@threshlabs.com.
