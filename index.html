Thresh Labs makes small, careful software for people who get paid on a cycle. One question runs underneath everything we ship: between now and the next payday, what is actually yours to spend? We answer it one product at a time.

About

Built for the days between paychecks.

Thresh Labs LLC is a Missouri software company — not a financial institution. We don't move money, hold money, or give financial advice. Our products work from numbers you enter yourself, and those numbers stay yours.

The work is deliberately slow. Money software earns trust by being right every single time, so we'd rather ship one product that holds up than four that mostly do.
